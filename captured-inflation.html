Theory of Captured Inflation

A Macroeconomic Application of Persistence Science

Standard macroeconomic models assume price adjustments occur symmetrically. The Theory of Captured Inflation applies the Odero Axiom to demonstrate how asymmetric market control leads to structural price stickiness and purchasing power decay.

Core Principles

  • Asymmetric Price Transmission: When input costs decline, market power allows firms to retain higher price levels as captured margin.
  • The Odero Capture Gap: Formulates the structural divergence between falling producer prices and sticky consumer retail prices.
  • Systemic Control Breakdown: Models persistent inflation as a degradation of consumer purchasing control within market networks.
