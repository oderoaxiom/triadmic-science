Theory of Captured Inflation

A Macroeconomic Application of Persistence Science

Standard macroeconomic models assume price adjustments are symmetric. The Theory of Captured Inflation applies the Odero Axiom to demonstrate how pricing power and downward control collapse lead to asymmetric price persistence[cite: 3].

Theoretical Highlights

  • Asymmetric Price Transmission: When production costs fall, firms capture the gap as margin expansion rather than lowering retail prices[cite: 3].
  • The Odero Capture Gap: Formulates the structural divergence between producer index drops and consumer price rigidities[cite: 3].
  • Control Collapse: Demonstrates how persistent inflation reflects consumer purchasing control breakdown within economic networks[cite: 3].
