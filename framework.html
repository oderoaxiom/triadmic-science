Foundations

The foundational bedrock of Persistence Science defining control, information structuring, and efficiency ratios.

AXIOM
C(I) × E > D

Odero Persistence Axiom

A system persists if and only if its information-directed control capacity multiplied by available energy exceeds its total rate of decay. This establishes the universal boundary separating surviving organizations from systemic collapse. Without sufficient control or energy to offset decay, structural degradation becomes inevitable.

FOUNDATION
I = A × ηI

Information Index

Defines information not merely as data storage or uncertainty reduction, but as actionable control capacity. It scales raw structural organization by operational efficiency to quantify true system agency. Higher index values reflect a greater capacity to direct energy against entropic pressures.

RATIO
Ω = (C(I)E)/D

Persistence Ratio

Synthesizes control, energy, and decay into a single dimensionless scalar metric. When the ratio remains strictly above unity, the system maintains structural integrity and evolutionary viability. Values dipping toward or below unity indicate critical systemic vulnerability and imminent collapse.
