Triadmics Science

A Domain-Invariant Mathematical Framework for System Continuity

Every complex system in existence—from physical matter and biological organisms to macroeconomic markets and digital networks—is constantly subject to environmental decay and operational entropy. Triadmics Science establishes a first-principles foundation to analyze how complex systems preserve structural integrity over time[cite: 1, 2, 3].

The Foundational Odero Axiom
$$C(I) \cdot E > D$$
Information-directed Control ($C(I)$) $\times$ Usable Energy ($E$) > System Decay ($D$)[cite: 1, 2, 3]

The Mathematics of Persistence

Systemic continuity relies on the precise balance of three foundational variables:

  • Information-Directed Control ($C(I)$): The structural algorithms, feedback loops, and regulatory logic governing how a system organizes work[cite: 1, 2, 3].
  • Usable Energy ($E$): The operational capacity required to carry out essential work and maintain order[cite: 1, 2, 3].
  • System Decay ($D$): The cumulative friction, entropy, and environmental breakdown pulling the system toward structural failure[cite: 1, 2, 3].

Persistence Capacity ($P$): Defined as $P = C(I) \cdot E$[cite: 2].

Persistence Index ($\Omega$): Calculated as $\Omega = \frac{P}{D}$[cite: 2].

  • $\Omega > 1$: System growth, adaptation, and structural resilience[cite: 2].
  • $\Omega = 1$: Systemic equilibrium.
  • $\Omega < 1$: Irreversible control collapse and structural decay[cite: 2].
