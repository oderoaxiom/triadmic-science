Persistence Science

A Unified Framework for Understanding Persistence, Adaptation, and Collapse Across Complex Systems

Persistence Science is a first-principles scientific framework for understanding why systems survive, adapt, transform, or collapse over time. It establishes a common mathematical language for studying persistence across physical, biological, economic, political, technological, and cosmological systems.

The Foundational Axiom

C(I) × E > D
A system persists if and only if its information-directed control capacity multiplied by available energy exceeds its total rate of decay.
C(I)
Information-Control Capacity
E

D
Decay
Persistence emerges when information successfully directs energy against decay.
