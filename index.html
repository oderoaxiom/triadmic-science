Triadmics Science

A First-Principles Foundation for Complex System Resilience

Every complex open system—whether a biological organism, market architecture, physical universe, or digital network—operates under constant thermodynamic and operational degradation. Triadmics Science establishes a unified mathematical framework explaining how systems preserve structural identity over time.

The Foundational Odero Axiom
$$C(I) \cdot E > D$$
Information-directed Control ($C(I)$) $\times$ Usable Energy ($E$) > System Decay ($D$)

The Mathematics of Persistence

A system maintains continuity through the interaction of three fundamental parameters:

  • Information-Directed Control ($C(I)$): The operational rules, algorithmic pathways, and feedback loops directing internal order.
  • Usable Energy ($E$): The active energy required to perform work and offset internal friction.
  • System Decay ($D$): The total background entropy, wear, and structural erosion.

Persistence Capacity ($P$): Defined as $P = C(I) \cdot E$.

Persistence Index ($\Omega$): Measured as $\Omega = \frac{P}{D}$.

  • $\Omega > 1$: System growth and long-term durability.
  • $\Omega = 1$: Dynamic balance point.
  • $\Omega < 1$: Systematic decay and structural breakdown.
