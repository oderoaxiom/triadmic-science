Support Persistence Science Research

Sustaining Long-Term Discovery, Open Access, and Systems-Based Inquiry

Persistence Science is an independent research initiative dedicated to exploring how sustained effort, resilience, information dynamics, and long-term commitment contribute to scientific discovery, innovation, and sustainable development.

Through mathematical modeling, continuous research, and open-access knowledge sharing, this work generates evidence-based tools and frameworks that help researchers, educators, and organizations build durable systems capable of long-term progress.

Independent Science Requires Sustainable Funding
Your contributions directly sustain data collection, mathematical modeling, analytical paper development, and open-access publishing without subscription paywalls or short-term commercial constraints.

Why Your Support Matters

Long-term, foundational research often struggles under traditional grant structures, which prioritize fast, short-term outcomes. Independent, crowd-backed funding allows for deep, persistent investigation into complex challenges.

Your support directly enables:

  • Ongoing Research: Funding data collection, analysis, framework modeling, and peer-reviewed publishing.
  • Open Knowledge Expansion: Keeping research papers, findings, and tools freely accessible to researchers globally.
  • Practical Tool Development: Creating actionable frameworks, guides, and analytical models for practitioners.
  • Collaborative Networks: Fostering cross-disciplinary partnerships that drive progress in complex systems theory.
