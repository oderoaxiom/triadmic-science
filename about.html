Biography & Research Focus

John Otieno Odero is an independent researcher and systems thinker based in Nairobi, Kenya. He is the founder of Triadmics Science, a unified mathematical and conceptual framework designed to analyze complex physical, economic, and organizational systems through the unified metrics of control, energy, and entropy.

By synthesizing principles from cybernetics, thermodynamics, information theory, and network physics, his work seeks to build first-principles solutions to long-standing problems across disparate fields—from cosmological expansion models to structural economic inflation.
