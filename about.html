John Otieno Odero

Independent Researcher | Systems Thinker | Founder of Persistence Science

John Otieno Odero is a graduate of Economics from the University of Nairobi, an independent researcher, systems thinker, and the founder of Persistence Science based in Nairobi, Kenya. His work investigates the fundamental principles governing how information, energy, and entropy interact to sustain complex open systems across physical, economic, and organizational domains.

Synthesizing quantitative methods from economics, cybernetics, non-equilibrium thermodynamics, and statistical modeling, his research formalizes mathematical models—such as the Odero Axiom ($C(I) \cdot E > D$), the Persistence Index ($\Omega$), and the Odero Capture Gap—that quantify structural durability against entropic degradation.

Research Focus & Theoretical Contributions

  • Foundational Persistence Science: Developing mathematical criteria for system continuity and resilience thresholds via the Odero Persistence Framework (Zenodo: 10.5281/zenodo.21841446).
  • Theoretical Cosmology: Formulating the Elasticosmos Regenesis Theory (ERT) to address singularity dynamics and cosmic energy continuity (Zenodo: 10.5281/zenodo.21650616).
  • Macroeconomics & Control Theory: Modeling market power capture and asymmetric price dynamics through the Theory of Captured Inflation and the Odero Capture Gap (Zenodo: 10.5281/zenodo.21323744).

Methodological Approach

Combining economic modeling, statistical analysis, computational methods in R, and cybernetic systems theory to construct cross-disciplinary frameworks verified against empirical data.
