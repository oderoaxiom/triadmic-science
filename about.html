Founder

John Otieno Odero

Founder of Persistence Science
Independent Researcher | Systems Thinker

John Otieno Odero is an independent researcher, systems thinker, and founder of Persistence Science, an interdisciplinary research framework investigating the principles governing persistence, adaptation, transformation, and collapse in complex systems. A graduate of Economics from the University of Nairobi, his work focuses on the interaction of information, control, energy, and decay across physical, economic, political, technological, and cosmological domains.

His research program is organized around the Persistence Science Framework, comprising foundational principles including the Odero Persistence Axiom, Information Index, and Persistence Ratio; core theories including Persistence Thermodynamics and System Dynamics & Control Theory; and application theories spanning economics, political science, and cosmology.

Notable contributions include the Odero Persistence Axiom, Information Index, Persistence Ratio, Thermoeconomic Information Theory, Theory of Captured Inflation, Coalition Indispensability Theory, the Fundamental Cosmological Equation, and Elasticosmos Regenesis Theory. Collectively, these works seek to establish a unified scientific framework for understanding how systems survive, adapt, and evolve across diverse domains of inquiry.
