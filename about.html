Academic Profile & Background

John Otieno Odero is a graduate of Economics from the University of Nairobi and an independent researcher based in Nairobi, Kenya. He is the founder of Triadmics Science, a theoretical framework analyzing system continuity across physical, economic, and organizational domains.

Synthesizing methods from quantitative economics, cybernetics, and non-equilibrium thermodynamics, his research focuses on universal failure metrics and systemic control preservation.
