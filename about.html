Academic & Professional Background

John Otieno Odero is a graduate of Economics from the University of Nairobi and an independent researcher based in Nairobi, Kenya[cite: 1, 2, 3]. He is the founder of Triadmics Science, an emerging theoretical framework analyzing how information-directed control, available energy, and systemic decay govern complex open systems[cite: 1, 2, 3].

Drawing on quantitative methods, thermodynamics, cybernetics, and network economics, his work focuses on deriving first-principles solutions to domain-invariant failure signatures across macroeconomic structures and cosmological dynamics[cite: 1, 2, 3].
