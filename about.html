About Persistence Science

A unified scientific framework investigating the principles governing persistence, adaptation, transformation, and collapse in complex systems.
Founder

John Otieno Odero

Founder, Persistence Science Framework
Independent Systems Researcher

John Otieno Odero is the founder of the Persistence Science Framework and an independent systems researcher based in Nairobi, Kenya. Persistence Science is a scientific framework for understanding how information, control, energy, and decay influence the persistence, adaptation, transformation, and collapse of complex systems.

His work focuses on developing a common scientific language for studying persistence across physical, economic, institutional, political, religious, technological, biological, and cosmological domains.

The Persistence Science library currently includes foundational frameworks, core theories, and application frameworks spanning economics, policy and institutions, religious systems, political systems, and cosmology.

Current work focuses on validation methodologies, comparative persistence measurement, institutional legitimacy, organizational trust, and large-scale applications of Persistence Science across both social and physical systems.

The long-term objective of Persistence Science is to establish persistence as a unifying scientific principle and to foster an open community of researchers exploring persistence across domains.

Mission

Mission

Persistence Science seeks to establish a common scientific language for understanding why systems persist, adapt, transform, or collapse.

The framework applies across physical, institutional, economic, political, religious, technological, and cosmological systems.

Current Development

The Next Frontier

Persistence Science is expanding beyond institutional systems toward one of the largest persistence questions in science: the Universe itself.

Current development includes:

  • Biological Persistence
    Investigating adaptation, metabolic constraints, and evolutionary longevity thresholds across living systems.
  • Cosmological Persistence & Hubble Constant
    A Persistence Science investigation of cosmic expansion, dark energy, and large-scale cosmological evolution.
  • Persistence Science Validation Framework
    Establishing standardized methods for measuring Information Control, Persistence Capacity, Legitimacy, Trust, and Organizational Decay.
  • Global Persistence Index & Institutions
    Comparative measurement of institutional legitimacy, organizational trust, and governance resilience.
  • Cross-Domain Persistence Studies
    Testing whether common persistence principles govern economies, institutions, religious systems, and cosmological structures.
