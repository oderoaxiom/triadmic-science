APPLIED PHYSICS & SYSTEM DYNAMICS

Applied Physics Framework

Continuous State-Space Formulation & Control Theory Dynamics

Extending the Odero Persistence Axiom into continuous physical domains. This framework models physical systems through state-space formulations, tracking the persistence index trajectory $\Omega(t)$, velocity ($\omega$), acceleration ($\alpha$), and control-phase simplex ($\kappa$).

Continuous Control Dynamics
$$\frac{d\Omega}{dt} = \frac{\dot{C}(I)E + C(I)\dot{E}}{D} - \Omega \frac{\dot{D}}{D}$$
Governing the rate of change of systemic resilience under fluctuating energy inputs and decay rates.
Omega Velocity ($\omega$)
$$\omega = \frac{d\Omega}{dt}$$
Omega Acceleration ($\alpha$)
$$\alpha = \frac{d^2\Omega}{dt^2}$$

System Dynamics & Phase Space

Physical systems maintain structural stability by continuously compensating for entropy. When tracking systems through control phase-space ($\kappa$), stability thresholds dictate whether a system converges toward steady-state persistence or undergoes rapid structural collapse.

  • Information-Control Coupling: How feedback loops optimize energetic distribution.
  • Dissipation Boundaries: Critical thresholds where decay rates ($D$) overwhelm control capacity ($C(I) \cdot E$).
  • Phase Simplex Mapping: Multidimensional tracking of systemic health metrics over chronological time.
