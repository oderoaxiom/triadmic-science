Cyclic Cosmological Model

Elasticosmos Regenesis Theory (ERT)

Proposes that cosmic spacetime possesses elastic tension. Cosmic expansion reaches a maximum critical strain point, triggering a non-singular bounce and continuous energy regenesis without a destructive singularity.

Core Principles:
  • The Universe May Be Regenerative Rather Than Terminal: Cosmic evolution may proceed through recurring phases of transformation and renewal rather than ending in a final state.
  • Cosmic Singularities and Runaway Expansion May Be Avoided: An intrinsic elastic response of spacetime may regulate cosmic extremes, preventing both physical singularities and unbounded expansion.
$$\mathcal{T}_{\text{elastic}} = \kappa \cdot \left(\frac{a}{a_0} - 1\right) - \gamma \cdot \dot{S}_{\text{regen}}$$

Elastic stress tensor model linking cosmic scale factor ($a$), elasticity constant ($\kappa$), and entropy dissipation ($\gamma \cdot \dot{S}_{\text{regen}}$).

Fundamental Cosmological Equation (FCE)

  • Dark Energy Realization: Formulates cosmic acceleration as an emergent phenomenon driven by underlying information field dynamics.
  • Resolution of $H_0$ Discrepancy: Models metric shifts and information coupling $C(\phi)$ across cosmic epochs.
  • Information & Persistence Drivers: Mathematically integrates information-mediated processes into cosmic energy balance.
$$\mathcal{E}_{\text{cosmo}} = \frac{3H^2}{8\pi G} + \rho_{\text{m}} + \frac{k}{a^2} + \Lambda(t) - \sigma_{\text{entropy}}$$

Dynamic balance equation relating metric expansion ($H$), density ($\rho_{\text{m}}$), curvature ($k/a^2$), dark energy ($\Lambda$), and entropy ($\sigma$).

Theoretical Framework & Cosmological Implications

Under traditional cosmological models based on general relativity, the universe begins at a point singularity (the Big Bang) and approaches maximum entropy or eternal accelerated expansion. The Applied Cosmological Model under Persistence Science reconciles these boundary issues by integrating non-equilibrium thermodynamics and elastic continuum mechanics into cosmic dynamics.

By linking the Fundamental Cosmological Equation directly to Elasticosmos Regenesis Theory (ERT), the model establishes three key principles:

  1. Dynamic Dark Energy ($\Lambda(t)$): Dark energy is treated as a time-dependent function of spatial elasticity and system-wide entropy production rather than an immutable vacuum constant.
  2. Regenerative Cyclic Bounce: Spacetime elasticity creates a restoring force at cosmic extremes, preventing infinite expansion and singularity formation during contraction.
  3. Thermodynamic Persistence: Cosmic structures persist across state transitions by continuously shedding entropy through phase boundaries, matching the broader principles of Persistence Physics and the Odero Axiom.
