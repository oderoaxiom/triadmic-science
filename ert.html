Applying Persistence Science to Cosmology

Standard cosmological models rely on dark energy and mathematical singularities (like the Big Bang center) to explain why the universe expands. Elasticosmos Regenesis Theory (ERT) applies the Odero Axiom ($C(I) \cdot E > D$) to reframe space-time as an active, elastic fluid system governed by information and energy flows.

Key Core Concepts

  • Layered Space-Time Currents: Rather than space stretching uniformly, space-time moves in localized directional flows (currents).
  • Redshift Duality: Light stretching across long distances is caused by interactions with space-time tension and energy gradients, offering a simpler explanation for redshift anomalies without invoking speculative forces.
  • Entropy-Driven Regenesis Nodes: Resolves the Big Bang singularity by demonstrating that when decay ($D$) approaches maximum thresholds, energy undergoes localized recycling (regenesis) through equilibrium nodes.
